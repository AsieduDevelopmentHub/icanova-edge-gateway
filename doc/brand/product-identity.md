## iCaNova Edge Gateway

Embedded industrial edge gateway (product family **iCaNova Edge**) for connecting and monitoring industrial equipment through supported interfaces (RS485 / Modbus RTU and related local APIs).

Physical direction (when enclosure branding is applied): matte graphite, minimal silver or white markings, clear terminal labels. Electrical design is unchanged by branding.

## iCaNova Monitoring

Web platform for industrial equipment monitoring, device management, and operational visibility. Current implementation includes org-scoped fleet views, Auth0/OIDC sign-in, Convex backend, and local gateway Configure/Monitor tools.
